FAQ

Got questions?

Here are a few answers to help you get ready for your next adventure.

Which game is QuestCompass made for?

QuestCompass is an addon for WoW Forever. Check compatibility with your game client version before installing.

How do I install the addon?

When checkout is available, download the Windows launcher from the payment confirmation, choose your WoW Forever folder, and install QuestCompass. The GPLv3 addon and source remain publicly available under their license.

What does the paid service include?

After checkout launches, a one-time payment will provide the Windows launcher and 30 or 90 days of addon patch delivery and support. The GPLv3 addon and its source remain publicly available; checkout is not live yet.

Will the addon stop working when my service expires?

No. GPLv3 copies you've downloaded continue to work. The paid service covers update delivery through the official launcher and support; it does not disable the addon or restrict your rights under the GPL.
